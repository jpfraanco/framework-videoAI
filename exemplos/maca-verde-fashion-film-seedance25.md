# Maçã verde · fashion film de 30 s no Seedance 2.5 (exercício Human Academy)

Resposta ao pedido 5 do curso: animar a foto da menina no Seedance 2.5, 30 s, 1080p, multi-shot. Brief original em [fontes/human-academy-curso.md](../fontes/human-academy-curso.md).

Teoria por trás: [FRAMEWORK.md, seção 6.3](../FRAMEWORK.md#63-formato-seedance-25-seções-rotuladas-até-30-s).

## Traduzindo o brief

O brief é todo de sensação. O trabalho é transformar cada sensação em instrução de câmera, tempo ou física.

| No brief | No prompt |
|---|---|
| "cool, fashion film contemporâneo" | Grade editorial limpa, 35 mm com grão leve, pose de modelo sem posar demais, olhar para a lente em momentos escolhidos |
| "surreal" | Uma regra física só e repetível: a maçã se multiplica **uma vez**, no ápice, e as cópias flutuam idênticas |
| "movimentos de câmera dinâmicos" | Push-in, worm's eye, órbita, travelling lateral: um movimento diferente por plano |
| "cortes criativos" | 7 planos com hard cuts, um corte no ápice do arremesso, montagem no ritmo dos gestos |
| "pausas dramáticas" | Duas pausas: a maçã parada no ápice em slow motion com o som caindo, e o congelamento antes da escolha |
| "sensação de scroll" | As maçãs se alinham em fileiras na altura dos olhos; cada gesto do dedo faz as fileiras deslizarem na horizontal e pararem com um leve quique elástico, como um feed; câmera acompanha de lado |
| "hiper-realista" | Física da maçã (massa, gravidade), brilho especular, suco na mordida, bloco anti-plástico |
| "divertida, impactante" | Fecho com mordida crocante olhando para a lente e sorriso de canto |

## Prompt (inglês, pronto para colar)

```
GLOBAL STYLE: Contemporary fashion film, cool, playful and surreal, hyper-real photorealism. Shot on 35mm film, fine grain, gentle halation on highlights, real lens character. Editorial color, 60:30:10: 60 clean bright sky blue and soft whites, 30 the girl's wardrobe tones exactly as in the start image, 10 vivid apple green; the green apples are the most saturated thing in every frame. 9:16 vertical. 24fps, 180 degree shutter, one speed ramp into deep slow motion (120fps played at 24fps). NOT 3D render, NOT CGI sheen, NOT cartoon. No text, no logos, no subtitles, no other people.

SCENE: A girl plays with a green apple, tosses it into the sky, it surreally multiplies into hundreds of floating apples, she scrolls through them in the air like a feed and picks one.

CHARACTERS: THE GIRL, from the start image: identity, face, hair, makeup, wardrobe and accessories exactly as in the start image in every shot, zero drift, natural skin with visible pores, no smoothing. Fashion-model poise, confident and playful, always reacting, never frozen in a pose.

PROPS: THE APPLE: one glossy Granny Smith green apple, about 8 cm, fine natural speckles, a short brown stem with one small leaf. Every multiplied apple is an identical copy, same size, same green, same stem and leaf.

LOCATION: the same place as in the start image, extended naturally, with open bright sky above her. Background kept simple and graphic, fashion-editorial.

FIRST FRAME AND BLOCKING: the video opens exactly on the start image. The girl as shown, the green apple resting in her right hand at chest height.

FORMAT MODE: Seven shots, six hard cuts, 30.0 seconds total. Playful fashion-film cutting with two held dramatic pauses.

Shot 1 (0.0s to 4.0s): Medium shot, 47 degree lens, slow push-in, light handheld drift. She rolls the apple across the back of her fingers, flicks it a few centimeters up and catches it with a soft slap in her palm, then glances at the lens with a half smile. Hard cut.

Shot 2 (4.0s to 6.0s): Worm's-eye close-up, 84 degree lens, camera below her hand pointing at the sky. Her arm whips up and throws the apple straight into the sky; the apple shoots out of the top of the frame. Hard cut.

Shot 3 (6.0s to 11.0s): Wide sky shot, 29 degree lens, static. The apple rises into clean blue sky and RAMPS TO DEEP SLOW MOTION at the apex, hanging almost still for a long beat, slowly spinning, sun flaring behind it. At 8.5s it splits into two, four, sixteen, then hundreds of identical green apples, blooming outward across the whole sky in a spherical burst. SNAPS BACK to real time as the burst spreads. Hard cut.

Shot 4 (11.0s to 15.5s): Low-angle wide, 84 degree lens, slow orbit around her. She stands beneath a sky full of hundreds of green apples floating and gently bobbing, small shadows dotting the ground; she looks up, amazed, and laughs. Hard cut.

Shot 5 (15.5s to 22.0s): Side medium shot, 47 degree lens, lateral tracking. The apples settle into neat horizontal rows at her eye level. She raises her index finger and swipes sideways; the rows glide horizontally past her like a scrolling feed and ease to a stop with a subtle elastic bounce. Three swipes, at 16.5s, 18.5s and 20.5s, each one faster; the camera tracks sideways matching each scroll. Hard cut.

Shot 6 (22.0s to 25.5s): Close-up, 29 degree lens, static. She stops, finger held in the air. Every apple freezes in place. A held pause: she tilts her head, eyes scanning the rows, a tiny knowing smile, then points at one apple, which drifts forward out of its row toward her. Hard cut.

Shot 7 (25.5s to 30.0s): Hero close-up, 29 degree lens, slow push-in. She plucks the chosen apple from the air, lifts it beside her face and takes one crisp bite, looking straight into the lens with a confident smirk; fine juice droplets catch the light. Behind her, all the other apples drop out of frame at once. The last second holds on her face, the lower third of the frame kept clean.

OPTICS: 47 degree normal lens for medium shots, 29 degree short telephoto for close-ups with the sky melting into soft bokeh, 84 degree wide for low angles, no fisheye distortion.

PHYSICS: The apple has real mass: on the toss it decelerates under gravity to the apex. Floating apples bob slowly in a gentle sine drift, never collide, never change size. The scroll rows move as rigid lines with an elastic ease-out. Her hair and clothes move with real fabric weight on every gesture. The bite breaks the skin with a crisp bite mark and a fine spray of juice.

LIGHTING: Bright natural daylight, sun high behind her from frame right, rim light on her hair, soft sky fill on her face. Every apple carries a small specular highlight from the sun. No artificial lights.

AUDIO: Stylized diegetic sound only. A soft slap of the apple in her palm, an air whoosh on the throw, near silence at the slow-motion apex with a faint high ring, a crisp rippling pop cascade as the apples multiply, a soft whoosh and tick for each scroll swipe, a loud crunchy bite at 27.5s. No music, no dialogue, no narration, no subtitles.

POSITIVE LOCKS: Her identity and wardrobe identical to the start image in every shot. Every apple identical. The apples multiply only once, at 8.5s. Only the girl appears. Nothing readable anywhere.
```

## Comando

Confira antes o esquema ao vivo do modelo (os parâmetros do Seedance 2.5 ainda não estão no MODELS.md da CLI):

```bash
higgsfield model get seedance_2_5
```

Depois gere. Se o modelo pedir um `--mode` para imagem de partida, use o valor que o `model get` mostrar (o README só mostra `t2v`, para texto):

```bash
higgsfield generate create seedance_2_5 --prompt "$(cat maca-verde.txt)" --start-image ./menina.png --aspect_ratio 9:16 --duration 30 --resolution 1080p --bitrate_mode high --wait
```

## Como economizar

1. Rode primeiro uma versão de 10 s só com os planos 2 e 3 (o arremesso e a multiplicação). É o efeito mais difícil. Se ele funcionar, o resto funciona.
2. Depois gere os 30 s completos.
3. Se só um trecho errar (uma maçã com tamanho diferente, a mão no scroll), use o region edit em vez de refazer.
4. Proporção: 9:16 porque é peça de rede social. Se a foto de partida for horizontal, troque para 16:9 e mude "lower third of the frame kept clean" conforme o espaço da marca.
